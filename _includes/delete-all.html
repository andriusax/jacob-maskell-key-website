{% comment %} "delete all" easter egg, triggered by the button in the navbar's Video column. Jacob's face stays where it is in the homepage photo (eyes still following the cursor until they roll back to white) and his mouth slowly unhinges into a huge toothless hole: the jaw drops far too far and the lips stretch, and everything on the screen is sucked into it in a spiral. After a moment of silence he retches it all back out and his face settles back to normal. The photo is warped live on a canvas laid over the lower half of the photo (the eyes above it are untouched, so they keep following the cursor). The mouth position is measured from this specific photo, like the eyes. On pages without the photo, the face appears in the middle of the screen instead. Nothing is really deleted: elements are only moved with CSS transforms and their original inline styles are put back at the end. Esc skips to the end. {% endcomment %}
